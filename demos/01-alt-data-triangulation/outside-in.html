Every claim gets two numbers, not one. Evidence is which way the data points. Confidence is whether we are entitled to an opinion at all — which depends on how much independent evidence there is, not how loud any single source is. Select a claim to see what sits underneath it.

Where the evidence actually comes from

Claims down the side, independent source families across the top. Two review sites agreeing is one family, not two — which is why a row that looks well-evidenced can still carry low confidence. The gaps matter as much as the colours.

The growth claim, bounded from filings alone

You cannot see same-store revenue from outside. But you can see filed group accounts, and you can see every acquisition and its completion date at Companies House. That is enough to bound the organic rate without a single line of management data.

The arithmetic

Let r be revenue per legacy practice-month and α the size of an acquired practice relative to a legacy one. Then:

R₀ = r · (LPM₀ + α·APM₀) R₁ = r · (1+g) · (LPM₁ + α·APM₁) R₁(LPM₀ + α·APM₀) 1+g = ———————————————————— R₀(LPM₁ + α·APM₁)

Everything on the right is observable except α. So the honest answer is a band, and the width of that band is itself a finding — it tells you exactly which single number to ask management for first.

    How it is built

    Source register

    Every source carries an explicit reliability prior and a feed latency. Nothing is weighted by vibe, and the weights are visible so a client can argue with them.

    Scoring

    Each signal is compared over a six-month window against the same window a year earlier, peer-adjusted where a peer series exists, then standardised by the standard error of that difference — not by month-to-month noise, which is roughly six times too large and would crush every signal toward zero.

    w = reliability × recency × sample adequacy E = Σ(w·d) / Σw evidence, −1 to +1 C = |Σ(w·d)| / Σ(w·|d|) coherence F = 1 − e^(−0.9(families−1)) independence M = 1 − e^(−Σw / 1.6) evidence mass confidence = M × √F × √C

    All three must hold. A single authoritative source cannot produce a confident verdict on its own, and two sources that disagree cannot either — which is the whole reason the framework exists rather than a weighted average.

    About this data

    Kestrel Veterinary Group is synthetic. The generator models a real estate of practices with dated acquisitions, integration disruption, a staffing market and a pricing dynamic, then emits only what a subscription would actually return. The engine never sees the practice-level truth — it is used once, at the end, to check that the outside-in band actually contains the answer.
