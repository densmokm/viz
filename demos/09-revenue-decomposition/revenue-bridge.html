    How it is built

    Ingredients

    Everything here comes from one table. On a live deal this is a sales day-book extract or an ERP invoice-line dump — typically one overnight request.

      The arithmetic

      Price, volume and mix are defined so they sum to the movement exactly, with no residual to explain away:

      Volume = (Q₁ − Q₀) × p₀ Price = Σ q₁ × (p₁ − p₀) Mix = Σ q₁ × p₀ − Q₁ × p₀ ∴ Volume + Price + Mix ≡ R₁ − R₀

      Subscripts are prior and current year; p₀ is the prior-year average price across the book, p₀ per SKU. The customer lens partitions the same movement into accounts that left, arrived, grew and shrank. Both are computed on constant-currency revenue, with FX carried as a single translation line.

      About this data

      Meridian Coatings is synthetic. The generator models drivers — a cost index, contractual passthrough by segment, customer churn and acquisition hazards, product family drift, an FX path and one lumpy infrastructure contract — and emits invoice lines. The analysis then discovers the bridge from those lines without being told what it should find, which is the point: the same code runs unchanged against a real day-book.
